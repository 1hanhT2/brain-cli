# Writing feedback pillars

Evaluate only the selected pillar in one check.

## Cohesion

Check whether sentences and paragraphs connect logically. Point to one missing transition, unclear reference, abrupt shift, or especially effective connection.

## Grammar

Select one consequential or recurring grammar problem. Explain the pattern with a short correction; do not copy-edit every sentence.

## Task achievement

Compare the draft with the writer's stated purpose, audience, genre, and deliverable. Identify the single adjustment that most improves fulfillment of that goal.

## Content

Check the substance: specificity, evidence, reasoning, examples, insight, and relevance. Ask for or recommend one concrete addition, removal, or clarification.

## Organisation

Check ordering and structure across the draft. Focus on one misplaced paragraph, weak section boundary, missing stage, or effective structural decision.

## Nudge format

Return plain Markdown containing:

1. A short pillar label.
2. One observation grounded in the draft.
3. One next action the writer can apply without stopping their flow.

Stay below 90 words. Do not provide a full rewrite.
